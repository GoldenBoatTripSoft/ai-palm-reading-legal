Privacy Policy

AI Palm Reading · GoldenBoatTripSoft
Last updated: November 13, 2026

About this policy

GoldenBoatTripSoft provides AI Palm Reading (Palmistry Reader), an entertainment app for palm interpretations, tarot, astrology, horoscopes and reflective conversation. This policy explains the information used by these features and by the services integrated into the app.

Information you choose to provide

Your local profile can include a name, gender, birth date, birth time and a birthplace with coordinates and time zone. A birthplace is selected or entered by you; the app does not need to track your device’s GPS location to calculate a chart. Love compatibility uses the names or details you enter. Tarot selections, preferences, saved readings and chat history may be stored in the app’s private storage.

Camera, photos and AI conversations

Camera access is requested for scanning a palm. Android’s photo picker grants access to an image you select. To obtain an AI reading or answer, the selected palm image or submitted messages, language and relevant profile or reading context are transmitted to the configured processing service. That service uses OpenRouter and an AI model provider. Do not submit images or personal information about another person without permission. Palm images are used for the requested interpretation; the app does not provide biometric authentication or identity verification.

Online services and service providers

OpenRouter and the selected model provider process AI requests under their own privacy terms. Their logging and retention can depend on the provider and service configuration; we do not promise that all providers immediately delete every request. The app also requests daily zodiac content from FreeHoroscopeAPI using the selected zodiac sign. Birthplace search uses a bundled GeoNames-derived catalog. Translation features can use Google ML Kit and downloaded language models.

Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and an advertising wrapper that includes Firebase services, Meta SDKs and AppsFlyer. Depending on enabled services and your choices, these providers may process IP addresses, advertising or installation identifiers, device and app information, advertising interactions, usage events, crash diagnostics and notification tokens. These services support advertising, attribution, reliability and operation. Their policies and any applicable consent choices also govern their processing.

Payments

Google Play handles purchases and payment details. The app and its billing service use purchase identifiers, purchase tokens and subscription status to determine access and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment card details. The price, period, renewal and cancellation conditions are those shown by Google Play when you purchase.

Storage and retention

Profile details, saved results, selected photos, chat history and preferences can remain on your device until removed through available app controls or by clearing app storage. Device backup settings may also apply. Request data can exist in processing-service memory and in third-party systems for their operational, security and legal purposes. We retain support correspondence and other service records only as needed for the purpose for which they were received or applicable obligations. Retention by independent service providers follows their own policies.

Your choices and requests

You can change permissions, notification settings and advertising identifier settings through Android, and manage subscriptions through Google Play. Clearing app storage removes local app data; uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests concerning data held by us, contact support@goldenboat.io. We may need enough information to locate the relevant records. Rights and available remedies depend on your jurisdiction.

Security, international processing and children

We use reasonable safeguards and rely on service providers that may process information in other countries. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be risk-free. The service is not designed for young children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information should contact us. Use must also comply with the app store’s age requirements and applicable local rules.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated as features or service providers change. The date on this page identifies the published revision. Contact GoldenBoatTripSoft at support@goldenboat.io with questions about the app or this policy. The English version is the reference text; translated views are provided for convenience.
